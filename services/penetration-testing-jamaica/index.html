What Is Penetration Testing

A penetration test is an authorized attack simulation. It chains real weaknesses to show actual business impact, not just scanner output. We prioritize findings by exploitability and consequence.

Our Jamaica Pen Test Methodology

Our engagements follow scoped rules of engagement: reconnaissance, enumeration, safe exploitation, lateral movement checks, cleanup, and structured reporting with CVSS-aligned risk ratings.

Types of Testing

We perform external network, internal network, web application, mobile application, wireless, and social engineering assessments.

Reporting and Remediation Guidance

Deliverables include executive summaries, technical reproduction steps, prioritized remediation guidance, and optional retests after fixes.
