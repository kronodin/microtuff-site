Penetration Testing in Jamaica

Microtuff Solutions delivers controlled offensive security assessments for Jamaican organizations. We test networks, applications, and people like real attackers — with full transparency and business-focused reporting.

What Is Penetration Testing

A penetration test is an authorized attack simulation. It chains real weaknesses to show actual business impact, not just scanner output. We prioritize findings by exploitability and consequence.

Our Methodology

Every engagement follows scoped rules of engagement: reconnaissance, enumeration, safe exploitation, lateral movement checks, cleanup, and structured reporting with CVSS-aligned risk ratings.

Types of Testing

External and internal network, web applications, mobile apps, wireless, and social engineering assessments.

Reporting

Executive summaries, technical reproduction steps, prioritized remediation guidance, and optional retests after fixes.
