Red Teaming in the Caribbean

Microtuff Solutions delivers scenario-driven red team engagements from Jamaica. We test people, process, and technology under realistic pressure so you know whether your defenses would hold.

Red Teaming vs Penetration Testing

Penetration testing asks: can you break in? Red teaming asks: can you detect, respond, and recover? Our engagements use broader scope, stealth tradecraft, and objective-driven scenarios.

Caribbean Threat Context

Regional organizations face ransomware, business email compromise, and supply-chain attacks. A local red team reflects actual regional targeting, not generic global playbooks.

Engagement Structure

We offer focused red team, full red team, and purple team options. Each engagement includes detection validation, clear reporting, and optional retests.
