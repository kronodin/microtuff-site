Safety First ⚠️

Electrical Safety Warning

Solar systems produce high voltage DC that can cause serious injury or fire. Follow NEC code, use properly sized wire, install fuses/breakers on all conductors, and hire a licensed electrician for grid-tied connections.

Wire Sizing & Fusing

Undersized wiring overheats. Size conductors for 1.25× expected current. Fuse every positive conductor at the battery and between components. Use marine-grade or solar-rated cable for outdoor runs.

Solar & Electrical Glossary

Simple definitions for common terms on solar labels, invoices, and JPS net-billing paperwork.

Watt (W)

Unit of power. A 100W bulb uses 100 watts while on.

Kilowatt (kW)

1,000 watts. A 5 kW system produces 5,000 watts at peak sun.

Kilowatt-hour (kWh)

Unit of energy. JPS bills in kWh.

Volt (V)

Electrical pressure. Common solar banks: 12V, 24V, 48V.

Ampere (A)

Current flow. Wiring and fuses are sized by amps.

Amp-hour (Ah)

Battery capacity. A 100Ah battery delivers 5A for 20 hours.

PSI

Pounds per square inch. Used for water pressure and pipe fittings.

BTU

British Thermal Unit. Heat energy; useful for water-heating sizing.

Peak Sun Hours

Hours of 1,000 W/m² irradiance. Jamaica averages 5.0–5.5.

Inverter

Converts DC battery power to AC for appliances.

Charge Controller

Regulates panel-to-battery charging to prevent overcharge.

Grid-Tied

Connected to JPS lines. Allows net billing for excess energy.
