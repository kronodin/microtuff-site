Safety First ⚠️

Electrical Safety Warning: Solar systems produce high voltage DC that can cause serious injury or fire. Follow NEC code, use properly sized wire, install fuses/breakers on all conductors, and hire a licensed electrician for grid-tied connections.

Wire Sizing & Fusing

Undersized wiring overheats. Size conductors for 1.25× expected current. Fuse every positive conductor at the battery and between components. Use marine-grade or solar-rated cable for outdoor runs.

NEC Requirements

Disconnects: DC and AC disconnects required
Grounding: All frames and enclosures grounded
Labeling: Warning signs at all junction boxes
Inspections: JPS inspection for grid-tied systems

Solar & Electrical Glossary

Simple definitions for common terms on solar labels, invoices, and JPS net-billing paperwork.

TermDefinition
Watt (W)Unit of power. A 100W bulb uses 100 watts while on.
Kilowatt (kW)1,000 watts. A 5 kW system produces 5,000 watts at peak sun.
Kilowatt-hour (kWh)Unit of energy. JPS bills in kWh.
Volt (V)Electrical pressure. Common solar banks: 12V, 24V, 48V.
Ampere (A)Current flow. Wiring and fuses are sized by amps.
Amp-hour (Ah)Battery capacity. A 100Ah battery delivers 5A for 20 hours.
PSIPounds per square inch. Used for water pressure and pipe fittings.
BTUBritish Thermal Unit. Heat energy; useful for water-heating sizing.
Peak Sun HoursHours of 1,000 W/m² irradiance. Jamaica averages 5.0–5.5.
InverterConverts DC battery power to AC for appliances.
Charge ControllerRegulates panel-to-battery charging to prevent overcharge.
Grid-TiedConnected to JPS lines. Allows net billing for excess energy.
