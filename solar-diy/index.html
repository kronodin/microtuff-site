Solar Fundamentals

Photovoltaic (PV) systems convert sunlight into electricity using semiconductor materials. The core equation:

Watts = Volts × Amps
Daily Wh = Panel Watts × Sun Hours × System Efficiency (0.75)
Example: A 400W panel in Jamaica (5.5 peak sun hours) produces: 400W × 5.5h × 0.75 = 1,650Wh/day

Grounding & Bonding

Frame Ground:  6 AWG copper to ground rod
Neutral Bond:  Only at main inverter (off-grid) or main panel (grid-tied)
Lightning:     Surge Protective Device (SPD) on DC and AC sides

Safety & Electrical Codes

Legal Notice: Grid-tied systems in Jamaica require JPS approval and licensed electrician sign-off. Off-grid systems still require electrical inspection. Always follow NEC/IEC standards.
  • Disconnects: Install DC disconnects between panels and controller, accessible within sight of array.
  • Labeling: Label all DC conductors with voltage and current ratings. Use UV-resistant labels.
  • Ventilation: Lead-acid battery rooms require 1 sq ft of ventilation per 50Ah. LiFePO4 can be indoors.
  • Fire Safety: Keep batteries away from combustibles. Install smoke detectors. Have Class ABC fire extinguisher.
  • PPE: Insulated gloves, safety glasses, arc-flash rated gear for systems over 48V.
