Solar Fundamentals

Photovoltaic (PV) systems convert sunlight into electricity using semiconductor materials. The core equation:

Watts = Volts × Amps
Daily Wh = Panel Watts × Sun Hours × System Efficiency (0.75)
Jamaica Solar Irradiance: Average 5.0–5.5 peak sun hours across Manchester Parish. A 400W panel produces: 400W × 5.5h × 0.75 = 1,650Wh/day
